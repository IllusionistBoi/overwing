Know before the airport does.

The hour before a flight is the anxious one. Overwing watches your flight so you don’t have to: gate changes and delay alerts land on your phone the moment they’re filed, often before the departure board blinks. And once you’re in the air, the people picking you up follow the same live map you do, from pushback to touchdown.

Every flight leaves a mark.

Land, and the trip files itself. Airports become stamps, countries fill your map, aircraft join your collection, and your lifetime distance climbs toward the next tier, Bronze to Platinum. No spreadsheets, no manual logging. Your travel identity builds itself while you fly.

Remember how it felt.

A journal entry waits after every landing: the photos you took, the people in seat 11B, a line about the view. Label the trip Vacation or Business and find it again at a glance. Come December, Wrapped turns your year in the air into a story worth sharing.

A LETTER FROM THE TRAVELER

Overwing exists because I wanted a calm, private, genuinely beautiful place to keep my own journeys, and couldn’t find one. So I built it: one person, a desk in Dublin, late nights and far too much coffee.

There’s no growth team here, no roadmap committee, no ads to chase. Just an app shaped with care for people who love the window seat as much as I do.

Thank you for flying with Overwing.

Ronit · Dublin
