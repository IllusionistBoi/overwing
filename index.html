Good to know

Questions, answered.

Is Overwing free?
Yes. Live tracking, alerts, the travel passport, journal, Wrapped, all five widgets, and cloud backup are free. Overwing Pro (€4.99/year) adds unlimited manual flight updates and photo backup, and helps cover the aviation-data costs.
Do I need an account?
No. Overwing runs fully offline and never forces a sign-up. You only sign in with Google if you want to back up your flights and journal to your own private cloud, so they survive a new phone.
Is my data private?
Completely. No ads, no trackers, no data selling. Your data lives on your device unless you choose to sign in, and even then it’s in your own account, which you can delete at any time from Settings. The app doesn’t use your location.
Which flights can I track?
Any scheduled commercial flight. Add it by flight number, search a whole city pair by route, or scan the barcode on your boarding pass and it fills itself in.
How accurate are the alerts?
Overwing pulls from aggregated aviation data and live aircraft positions, so gate and delay changes often reach you before the airport’s own boards update. When a live position isn’t available, the map shows an honest estimate based on the route and schedule.
Does it work offline?
Yes. It’s local-first: your saved flights, passport, and journal all work with no connection. Live updates simply resume when you’re back online.
Will it drain my battery?
No. Background updates are throttled and batched, and the app doesn’t track your device location or run constant GPS. It’s built to sit quietly until there’s something worth telling you.
Can I track a friend’s or family’s flight?
Yes. Assign a companion to any flight and you’ll get their gate, delay, and landing alerts, perfect for knowing exactly when to leave for the airport pickup.
How do the tiers work?
Your first completed flight unlocks Bronze. Silver is 5 flights and 2,500 km, Gold is 15 and 10,000 km, and Platinum is 30 flights and 30,000 km. Historical flights you import count too.
Is there an iPhone version?
Not yet. Overwing is Android-first, built by one person. iOS may follow if there’s demand.
When does it launch?
Soon, on Google Play, starting with a closed beta. The fastest way to hear about it is to email and ask to join.
