Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 July 2026

Overwing is a personal flight tracker. It's local-first: your data lives on your device by default. A few optional features (account sign-in, cloud backup) and live flight lookups involve a small amount of data leaving your device. This explains what we collect, why, and your choices.

The short version

We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and we don't track you across other apps or websites. Most data stays on your phone. Cloud backup is optional and locked to your own account. You can delete your account and all cloud data from inside the app.

What we collect

  • Flights you add — flight numbers, routes, times, seat, booking reference. You enter these.
  • Journal entries — your notes, ratings, photos, and the travel companions you tag.
  • Contacts you add — name, phone number, and photo, only for people you choose to add as travel companions.
  • Profile — a display name and an optional profile photo you set.
  • Account (optional) — if you sign in with Google, your name, email, and Google profile photo.
  • Anonymous identifier — to securely fetch flight data, the app uses an anonymous identity even if you never sign in. It isn't linked to you unless you choose to sign in.
  • Diagnostics — minimal technical data needed to make the app work (e.g. rate-limit counters). No advertising or cross-app tracking.

How your data is used

  • On your device — to show your flights, journal, passport stats, widgets, and notifications. This is the default and works fully offline.
  • Live flight data — flight searches are sent through our secure server to the aviation data provider. Your identity is not shared with the provider.
  • Optional cloud backup — if you sign in, your structured data (flights, journal text, passport, contacts, profile name) is backed up to your private space so you can restore it on a new device. With Pro, your photos are backed up too. Everything is locked to your account.

Who processes your data

We use a few trusted providers to deliver the features you use:

  • Google Firebase — secure sign-in, cloud sync, and photo backup.
  • AeroDataBox (via our server) — flight schedules and status.
  • Google Play Billing — to process the optional Pro subscription. We never see your card details.
  • Map, weather & aviation data providers — for map tiles, live aircraft positions, weather, place names, and aircraft photos. These receive only flight and airport identifiers, never your personal data.

Permissions we request

  • Notifications — flight status alerts (gate changes, delays, landing, journal reminders).
  • Camera (optional) — only to scan a boarding-pass barcode when you choose to. The barcode is read on your device; the image is not stored or uploaded.
  • Contacts (optional) — only when you choose to add a travel companion from your phone.
  • Photos — chosen through the system photo picker, so we only ever see the specific photos you pick.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it for advertising. Data is shared only with the processors above, strictly to provide the features you use.

Retention & deletion

  • Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.
  • Delete account — see the steps below. This permanently deletes your cloud-synced data and your account.
  • Data you never backed up only ever existed on your device.

How to delete your Overwing account and data

To request deletion of your Overwing account and all data associated with it:

  1. Open the Overwing app on your device.
  2. Go to Settings → Account.
  3. Tap Delete account, then confirm.

Deletion happens immediately, there is no waiting period or additional retention. Deleted permanently: your cloud-synced flights, journal entries and photos, travel companions/contacts, profile name and photo, and the account itself (Google Firebase Authentication + Firestore + Storage). Nothing is kept after deletion completes; we hold no backup copy. Data that was never backed up to the cloud only ever existed on your device, and is removed the moment you uninstall the app. If you'd rather request this by email instead of using the in-app button, write to illusionistboi@proton.me from the email address linked to your account and we'll process the deletion manually.

Security

Cloud data is restricted so only your account can read or write it. API keys are held on our servers, never shipped inside the app. Sign-in uses Google's secure authentication.

Children

Overwing is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy; we'll change the date above and, for significant changes, note it in the app.
